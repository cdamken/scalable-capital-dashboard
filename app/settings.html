Save Scalable credentials

Credentials are stored locally at ~/.sc-api/profiles/<email>/credentials.json (mode 0600). No login or push approval happens here — that triggers automatically the next time you click Update Now on any page.

🔐 Session

Scalable's session cookies are HttpOnly. When they expire, just log in again above — one push approval and everything else carries over.

💾 Local data

Downloaded JSONs (inventory, transactions, wealth, etc.) live in DATA/. Deleting them does NOT clear your cookies — it just forces a re-fetch on the next update.
